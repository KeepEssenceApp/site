FAQ

Direct answers.

Wait—it deletes my saved links after 30 days? What if I need one later?

If you did not read or deep-dive a raw link in 30 days, you were not going to—and its summary line stays in your Daily Digest permanently. You can extend an item's lifespan up to one year, or click Keep to remove expiry completely for rare references.

No search and no folders? How do I find things?

KeepEssence is the filter before your permanent notes, not another long-term archive. Every distilled Note is a clean, self-contained artifact you can download and export anywhere, and your connected AI assistant can still retrieve past digests and notes as context during a Deep Dive.

How does connecting Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini via MCP work?

Paste one shared remote endpoint (https://keepessence.app/mcp) into your assistant's custom connector or MCP settings and approve access on KeepEssence's OAuth page. There are no static credentials or local command-line servers to manage, and you choose exactly which steps each assistant can run.

How is KeepEssence different from Readwise Reader or Burn 451?

Archival readers like Readwise Reader are built for permanent accumulation, where MCP mainly queries a growing backlog. Countdown tools like Burn 451 rely on a 24-hour read-or-burn timer while still maintaining an article vault. KeepEssence runs autonomous AI triage before you read, delivers a calm 9 PM Daily Digest, lets raw links expire quietly after 30 days, and saves permanent Notes only when you deep-dive.

Where does my data go when I connect an external AI assistant?

During OAuth authorization, KeepEssence states explicitly that item content is sent to the assistant you select. Permissions are scoped per assistant and device, and you can revoke any assistant in one click from Assistant Settings.

What does KeepEssence cost?

The core platform, built-in routing assistant (up to 200 items/day), and Bring Your Own Assistants over MCP + OAuth are free. An optional paid tier for hosted autonomous assistants—giving zero-setup automated briefs, nightly digests, and close reads without self-hosting—is planned post-v1.0.
