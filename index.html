Help me see clearly first, then I decide.

An assistant reads what you save first and tells you what it says and whether it is worth your time. Whether to keep it is up to you.

Join the beta

Hundreds saved and not one opened again? You are not lazy. Nobody took a first look for you.

How it works

  1. SavePaste a link, text or an image on the web; with the browser extension on a computer, one click saves.
  2. SeeWithin a few minutes the assistant writes four lines: a summary, a key fact, up to three key points, and a verdict.
  3. You decideWorth it: tap “Deep dive”. Not for you: mark “Not interested”. The daily digest wraps up each night.
Example

In a task queue, how to make a piece of work belong to one person

Summary
A “lease” lets a task belong to one person at a time, and it goes back to the queue when the lease runs out.
Key fact
Article · about 3,200 words.
Key points
  • Claiming comes with a time limit; when it runs out the task goes back.
  • A hand-in can be replayed: handing in the same piece twice keeps only one.
  • Only the holder can renew.
Verdict
Worth a deep dive

Daily digest

After 9 PM every night, what you saved today becomes one page: what is worth a deep dive, the rest, and a question to sleep on. When you have read it, tap “Read. Good night”.

Deep dive and notes

Pick the one that is worth it, talk it through with an assistant, and keep it as your own note. Notes have a quick find and can be downloaded as Markdown. On a day you keep a note, the calendar shows a gold dot.

Connect your own assistant

One MCP address connects the assistant you already use, if it supports MCP. No assistant of your own? No problem: the built-in assistant writes the analyses and the daily digest, and it is free during the beta.
