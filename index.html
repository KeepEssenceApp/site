Deep dive

Pick one or two a day and work them through.

An assistant can filter for you and read ahead for you. Whether to keep something, and what to keep, is yours alone to decide.

Step 1

Pick the one worth it

Tap Deep dive on a brief or in the Daily Digest and it joins your queue, safe from the 30-day clear-out. If you run a scheduled agent, it reads the whole thing ahead of you.

Step 2

Hand it to your assistant — not just to chat

Say “deep dive the one about X from today’s digest” and your assistant fetches it. From there you question it, push back, or have it do the work — your call.

Step 3

Say “save it as a note”

Once you’ve worked it through and say so, it becomes a permanent note. Come back to it whenever you like, or share it. Anything you don’t save expires as usual.

Things you can ask your assistant
“Turn the key points into a checklist” “Run the tests and show me what fails” “Get to the bottom of this detail” “Compare it with the one I saved last time” “Turn my take on this into a new article”

Ask for whatever you need. What it can do is limited only by your assistant.

Questions

Everything expires after 30 days. Will it clear out something I wanted?

No. Tap Keep on anything you want and it never expires. Anything in your deep-dive queue is protected too. What expires is the raw item — its one-line summary and the original link stay in your Daily Digest for good. The notes you keep are permanent and quick to find.

I don't have an AI assistant and I've never used MCP. Can I still use it?

Yes. During the beta you can use the built-in agent and connect nothing yourself. If you later want your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, you paste one address into its settings and tap Allow.

Is it free now? Will it cost money later?

It's free now. The core product and connecting your own assistant stay free. Only one thing will be paid later: using the built-in assistant instead of connecting your own. Your notes and digests can be exported at any time — nothing is locked in.

Who can see what I save?

Only you, and the assistants you explicitly allow. When you connect an assistant, a confirmation page says exactly what will be sent to it. Permissions are separate for each assistant and each device, and you can revoke any of them in one tap under Assistant settings. A shared monthly grid shows dots only — never any content.

Which devices does it work on?

Saving: iPhone Shortcut, the phone share sheet, the browser extension, the Mac menu bar and the web. Reading: the phone app (opening with the beta) and the web.
